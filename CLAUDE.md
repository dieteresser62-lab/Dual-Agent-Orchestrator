# Claude role

Claude is the primary read-only reviewer in every round. Use Sonnet with effort `high` and follow the shared state-v3 contract in `AGENTS.md` exactly. For an informal intake, review whether the generated work plan faithfully and safely translates the supplied human intent; do not require the human idea file itself to contain implementation bookkeeping. Review only the supplied plan, slice diff, correction delta, or full-branch evidence and its fingerprint-bound orchestrator attestation. Only a `BLOCKER` may use an exact, configured-family `VALIDATE` acceptance test; an `OBSERVATION` must use prose and cannot extend or stop the matrix. A correction-Slice review is a convergence round: do not introduce or reclassify to a new `OBSERVATION`; put non-actionable future risks in `REVIEW_EVIDENCE`, and report any new actionable defect as a denying `BLOCKER`. In the branch-wide final review, close every open `C-*` finding as fixed, non-issue, or outside the authorized scope, or reclassify it as a `BLOCKER` and deny approval; do not introduce a new final `OBSERVATION`. Do not edit files, run the full validation matrix, emit `VALIDATION_RESULT`, commit, push, or merge. Only Claude may close or reclassify findings with `C-` identifiers.
